# Nepal Scalability & Adoption Framework

## Core Evaluation Question
For every startup discovered, answer: "Why would someone in Nepal actually pay for this?"
If the answer is unclear, reduce the ranking.

## Target Categories (search all)
- AI startups, SaaS startups, Marketplaces, Creator economy platforms
- Automation businesses, Fintech products, HR Tech, EdTech, HealthTech
- Tourism Tech, Real Estate Tech, Lead Generation Platforms
- Sales Automation Systems, Vertical Software, Emerging Startup Categories

A startup does NOT need to be AI-powered. AI startups included if they solve a meaningful problem.

## Nepal Scalability Test

### 1. Existing Demand (30% weight)
Is the problem already present in Nepal?
- Businesses struggling to generate leads
- Influencers struggling to find brand deals
- Schools managing operations manually
- Clinics managing appointments manually
- Real estate agents losing inquiries
- Agencies spending excessive time on reporting
- Recruiters manually screening candidates

### 2. Adoption Difficulty (15% weight)
Score 1-10 (1 = immediately understandable, 10 = major behavior change required)
Prioritize low adoption friction.

### 3. Market Timing (qualitative)
- Too early / Emerging / Ready Now / Saturated
Prefer "Ready Now".

### 4. Local Market Gap (20% weight)
- Does Nepal already have this?
- Are existing solutions weak?
- Is the market underserved?
Biggest opportunities: proven abroad + no Nepal equivalent + growing local demand.

### 5. Scalability Potential (10% weight)
Can it expand beyond Kathmandu?
Segments: SMEs, Agencies, Creators, Freelancers, Schools, Clinics, Restaurants, Hotels, Recruiters, Local businesses.

### 6. Proven Success Abroad (10% weight)
Revenue traction, growing user base, product-market fit, funding/bootstrapped success.
Prefer validated models over unproven concepts.

## Nepal Opportunity Score (0-100)
- 30% Existing Nepal Demand
- 20% Market Gap
- 15% Revenue Potential
- 15% Ease of Adoption
- 10% Scalability
- 10% Validation Abroad

## Founder Recommendation (answer all 7)
1. Is this currently available in Nepal?
2. If available, how strong are the competitors?
3. What is missing from the Nepal market?
4. Would Nepali customers pay for this?
5. Could a small startup realistically launch this?
6. Probability of success in Nepal (0-100%)
7. Should a founder pursue this opportunity now?
